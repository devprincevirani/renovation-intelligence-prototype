What an estimate is

Renovation Intelligence gives estimates for planning. An estimate is not a contractor's bid, an offer to do work or a guaranteed price.

How the ranges are made

Each cost is shown as a Low and a High. They come from local installed rates and real products for the work we found in your photos and facts. We never show a single expected figure.

What an estimate cannot see

  • Anything hidden inside walls, floors, ceilings or systems.
  • Items that were not in your photos. These are marked unverified and left out of the total, which is then labeled partial.
  • The condition of the roof, heating and cooling, water heater and electrical panel. For these we give general guidance only, never a price.

What we do not give

We do not inspect houses, and we do not give advice on permits, building codes, insurance or safety. A licensed contractor or inspector should see the house before you commit to any work.
