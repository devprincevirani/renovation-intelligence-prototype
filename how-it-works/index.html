Three goals

A package is a goal, not a list of work

Essential

A functional refresh. Fix what must be fixed so the house works and looks cared for.

Worn carpet stays if sound. Failed hinges are replaced. Walls get paint where needed.

Standard Default view

A coordinated update. Modernize dated parts and keep what is sound.

Oak cabinets refinished, carpet replaced with LVP, new light fixtures in dated rooms.

Premium

A higher finish. Better materials and more function, without replacing parts only because they are old.

New cabinets and quartz tops. A working furnace is still kept, pending an inspection.

Where we stop

What an estimate is not

We plan the work. A contractor, an inspector and the city still have their part.

  • A contractor's bid or a fixed price
  • A home inspection
  • Permit, building code or safety advice
  • A price for the roof, furnace, water heater or panel
  • A claim about anything hidden in walls or floors
  • A single "expected" number
