What we collect

Your email, account type and password (stored only in scrambled form), the houses you enter, their photos and facts, and how you use the site.

Your photos

  • Stored privately. Only you and the people you share a link with can see them.
  • Location data is removed from every copy we show or share.
  • Used to make your estimate. How they may be used to improve photo reading is Sam's decision and will be stated here.
  • Never sold.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details never reach our servers.

Your choices

You can turn off a share link at any time, ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account.

In this prototype

Nothing leaves your browser. Accounts, waitlist entries and choices are kept in this browser's local storage, and you can clear them from the Demo panel.
