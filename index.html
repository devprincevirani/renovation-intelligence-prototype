Questions

Before you start

If something here is not clear, the estimate itself will say so too.

How close is the number?

It is a range. The Low and the High come from local installed rates and real products for each line. Where our evidence is weaker, the range is wider. Lines we could not confirm are listed apart, never hidden inside the total.

Is this a contractor's bid?

No. It is an estimate to plan with before you call a contractor. A contractor still needs to see the house, and anything hidden inside walls, floors or systems stays unknown until someone opens them.

What do I need to give you?

The address, the facts you would find on a listing (year built, size, bedrooms, baths, basement), and a photo of each room. Room sizes help but are optional.

What does it cost me?

Seeing the price for each package is free. The full presentation, with rooms, trades and products, is one payment for that house.

What happens to my photos?

They are stored privately and used only for your estimate. Location data is removed from every copy we show or share. You confirm that you have the right to use the photos when you submit.
